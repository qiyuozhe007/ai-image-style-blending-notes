Three ways to mix styles

  1. Prompt weighting in words: "mostly watercolor texture, with anime line art and cel shading on the face". Say which style controls which part.
  2. Reference image + style preset: upload a photo, pick a preset such as Anime or Digital Art, then describe the second style.
  3. Blend two references: give one image for content and one for style, and ask the editor to combine them.

Make the blend look intentional

  • Assign roles: one style for linework, the other for color and texture.
  • Keep the palette limited so the two styles feel unified.
  • Iterate with small edits ("more watercolor bleed in the background") instead of rewriting the whole prompt.
